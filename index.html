Why is it crucial to maintain or replace your roof?

Your roof is your property’s first line of defence against harsh weather, heat, wind, and rain. Over time, even the strongest roofs can show signs of wear. Common issues include:

Rust or corrosion Loose or damaged sheets Leaks and water ingress Outdated appearance

Ignoring these problems can lead to structural damage and costly repairs. Re-roofing with metal not only restores protection but also improves energy efficiency and long-term value.

How long does a metal roof last?

A professionally installed metal roof can last 40–70 years or more, depending on the material and maintenance. It’s one of the most durable roofing options available.

Is metal roofing noisy during rain?

Modern metal roofs are installed over insulation and sarking, which significantly reduces noise. In most homes a metal roof is no louder than any other roofing material during rain.

Can you install a metal roof over my existing roof?

In many cases yes, but we always assess the condition of your existing roof first. Our team will recommend the safest, longest-lasting approach for your home.

How long does a re-roofing project take?

Most residential re-roofing projects are completed within a few days to a week, depending on the size and complexity of the roof and the weather conditions.

Is metal roofing energy efficient?

Yes. Metal roofing reflects heat rather than absorbing it, helping keep your home cooler in summer and reducing energy costs throughout the year.

Do metal roofs require a lot of maintenance?

Metal roofs are low maintenance. A simple periodic inspection and clean is usually all that’s needed to keep your roof performing at its best for decades.
